Read01 — how I found your business

I look for local businesses whose customers already love them, with plenty of recent reviews, and whose website is not keeping up with that.

The signs are usually the same: slow on a phone, a browser warning that says not secure, a booking or call button that is hard to find, a footer that still says 2017.

Your reviews are already doing the selling. The website should not be the reason someone calls the next place on the list.

What I check before writing to you

  • How it loads and reads on a phone
  • Security (the padlock) and speed
  • How many taps it takes to call or book
  • Whether your best reviews show up anywhere on your own site
