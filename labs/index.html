Dionaea

        

          
The plant behind the portfolio's hero is a Venus flytrap written in ASCII and rebuilt every frame. Each lobe is a lens-shaped arc hinged on the petiole, with teeth fanned toward the tip. Opening and closing is one number per trap.

          
It has a full state machine, and it was there before the thesis was. The thesis was read out of it, not drawn into it. Two lines decide everything:

        

        

          
hero.jsexcerpt


// it does not hunt until it finishes growing
if (grow < 0.85) { BUG.st = "wait"; return; }

// it yields the turn to the visitor
if (CUR.vis && CUR.st === "alive") { BUG.t0 = t + 2.0; return; }

        

        

          
First it gives itself a shape, then it catches. And when you enter the site, your cursor is a bug, \#/, and the plant waits for you instead of its own fly. The specimen on this page runs the same geometry and the same timing.

Clio
Reader

        

          
A local-first reader that keeps what you read: highlights and notes tied to their exact passage, stored in SQLite on your machine and exported to Markdown, ready for Obsidian. The desktop reader runs on Windows and Linux; an Obsidian plugin comes next and Android after that.

          
The symptom. I wanted my Zettelkasten notes in my Obsidian vault while I read. I could not find a Windows reader I liked, much less one that played well with Obsidian. My notes kept ending up sealed inside each EPUB reader, and the EPUB plugins inside Obsidian never felt comfortable.
