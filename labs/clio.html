02 — why clioread once, keep it for good

    

      
You mark a great passage today. Months later, it is still one click away.

      

        
never lose a note
Every note knows where it came fromHighlights and notes stay tied to their exact passage, so you can always go back to the page.

        
yours
No account. No cloud.Your library and everything you write live on your computer, and it works offline.

        
portable
Ready for ObsidianYour notes export as plain Markdown files that drop straight into an Obsidian vault, or open in any editor.

        
one library
Reads what you already haveBooks, papers, notes and comics: EPUB, PDF, Markdown and CBZ in one place.
